# Project types: real sites to study, a direction, must-have sections (checked 2026-10)

Open only your type's section (`rg -n "^## " project-types.md`). Study the sites for principles (hierarchy, density,
type, colour, how they explain the product), never to copy them. Some sites block automated requests; open them in
a browser tool.

## SaaS and B2B product
- Study: https://linear.app (precise type, product as the hero) · https://attio.com (calm density, data-forward) ·
  https://cal.com (open-source honesty, clear pricing) · https://superhuman.com (a speed story told visually) ·
  https://granola.ai (warm, editorial for a tool) · https://www.notion.com (playful illustration with restraint)
- Direction: show the real product early (screenshots or a live demo, not abstract art), concrete outcomes in
  headlines, one sharp accent colour, dense information where buyers compare.
- Must have: product proof, how it works, integrations, pricing, security or trust, FAQ, a clear sign-up path.
- Components: shadcn/ui + Tailark or shadcnblocks sections; charts from shadcn charts.

## Developer tool
- Study: https://vercel.com · https://resend.com (monochrome, typographic, code as hero) · https://supabase.com ·
  https://raycast.com · https://railway.com · https://warp.dev · https://posthog.com (bold personality, long-form)
- Direction: code and terminal output are the imagery; monospace used for code only; docs-like clarity;
  install-in-one-command CTAs; dark themes only if the audience lives in them.
- Must have: a quickstart snippet, docs link in the nav, pricing, changelog, open-source or community proof.
- Components: shadcn/ui; code blocks with syntax highlighting (Shiki); Magic UI sparingly.

## AI product
- Study: https://www.anthropic.com (editorial, restrained) · https://elevenlabs.io (product sound and voice as proof)
  · https://runwayml.com (the outputs are the hero) · https://cursor.com (product in motion) ·
  https://www.perplexity.ai (answer-first simplicity)
- Direction: show real outputs, not sparkles and gradients; plain words for what the model does; honest limits;
  avoid "magic" language.
- Must have: real examples or a demo, use cases by role, pricing, privacy and data use, FAQ.

## E-commerce and direct-to-consumer brand
- Study: https://www.aesop.com (sensory, typographic, slow) · https://www.allbirds.com (material story) ·
  https://www.glossier.com (community, photography) · https://teenage.engineering (product as object, quirky
  system) · https://www.everlane.com (transparency) · https://ugmonk.com (craft, minimal)
- Direction: photography leads; product details (materials, sizing, care) beat slogans; fast filters; a checkout
  that feels safe.
- Must have: category pages with filters, product pages (gallery, variants, reviews, shipping and returns), cart,
  trust signals, search.

## Marketplace
- Study: https://www.airbnb.com (search-first, photo-led cards) · https://www.etsy.com · https://www.hipcamp.com
  (place and mood) · https://www.depop.com · https://contra.com
- Direction: search and filters are the hero; listing cards carry the information hierarchy; trust and safety
  visible; two audiences (buyers, sellers) with separate paths.
- Must have: search, filters, listing cards and detail pages, profiles, reviews, how it works for each side.

## Agency, studio or creative team
- Study: https://www.pentagram.com (work first, quiet frame) · https://basement.studio (bold, interactive) ·
  https://locomotive.ca (motion craft) · https://www.instrument.com · https://metalab.com · https://area17.com
- Direction: the work is the interface; one strong typographic idea; motion with purpose; case studies with
  outcomes, not adjectives.
- Must have: selected work, case studies (problem, approach, result), services, clients, team, contact.

## Portfolio or personal site
- Study: https://rauno.me (interaction craft) · https://paco.me (minimal, personal) · https://emilkowal.ski (motion
  details) · https://brittanychiang.com (clear developer portfolio) · https://www.joshwcomeau.com (warm, playful
  teaching)
- Direction: personality through one or two details, not decoration everywhere; fast; readable writing.
- Must have: who you are in one sentence, selected work or writing, contact, links to profiles.

## Local business, restaurant or hospitality
- Study: https://www.dishoom.com (story, place, warmth) · https://acehotel.com · https://tartinebakery.com ·
  https://bluebottlecoffee.com · https://noma.dk
- Direction: place and photography first; opening hours, location and booking within one tap on mobile; the
  menu as real text, not a PDF; local character in type and colour.
- Must have: hours, address and map, booking or ordering, menu or services, photos, contact, LocalBusiness JSON-LD.

## Healthcare or clinic
- Study: https://www.onemedical.com · https://www.oscarhealth.com (friendly clarity) · https://www.parsleyhealth.com
  · https://www.functionhealth.com · https://www.mavenclinic.com
- Direction: calm, warm, legible; plain language at a reading age of about 12; real clinicians' photos; trust and
  privacy stated plainly; strong contrast and large tap targets.
- Must have: services, how care works, practitioners, insurance or pricing, booking, privacy, FAQ, accessibility.

## Fintech or finance
- Study: https://mercury.com (premium calm) · https://ramp.com (outcomes and numbers) · https://wise.com
  (transparent fees, a live calculator) · https://monzo.com (friendly, bold colour) · https://www.brex.com
- Direction: precise numbers, transparent fees, interactive calculators; security as substance, not badges;
  conservative motion.
- Must have: product proof, pricing or fees, security and compliance, how it works, support.

## Education or courses
- Study: https://www.duolingo.com (character-led) · https://brilliant.org (learning by doing) ·
  https://www.khanacademy.org (mission, clarity) · https://www.masterclass.com (cinematic) · https://maven.com
- Direction: show the learning itself (a sample lesson), outcomes for the learner, instructor credibility.
- Must have: curriculum or outline, a sample, instructors, pricing, testimonials (real), FAQ.

## Nonprofit or community
- Study: https://www.charitywater.org (transparency, proof of impact) · https://www.givedirectly.org (evidence-led)
  · https://codeforamerica.org · https://www.mozilla.org
- Direction: real people and places; impact in numbers with sources; one clear way to help; humble tone.
- Must have: mission, impact, stories, donate or volunteer, transparency (reports, finances), contact.

## Real estate or property
- Study: https://www.themodernhouse.com (editorial listings) · https://www.compass.com · https://www.opendoor.com
  (simple flow) · https://www.sothebysrealty.com
- Direction: large photography, generous whitespace, map and search; listing facts scannable (price, beds, area).
- Must have: search with map, listing pages (gallery, facts, floor plan, location), agent contact, valuation request.

## Events or conference
- Study: https://config.figma.com · https://stripe.com/sessions · https://smashingconf.com
- Direction: date, place and ticket CTA above the fold; the speakers and the agenda are the content; a strong
  event identity that can carry over to slides and social cards.
- Must have: date and venue, tickets, speakers, schedule, venue and travel, sponsors, FAQ, Event JSON-LD.

## Editorial, blog or media
- Study: https://press.stripe.com (book-like craft) · https://pudding.cool (visual essays) · https://www.theverge.com
  (bold system) · https://every.to · https://www.theguardian.com
- Direction: typography is the design: reading measure 60–75 characters, a clear scale, generous line height;
  author and date visible; images with captions.
- Must have: article template, author pages, topics, search, newsletter, Article and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD.

## Documentation or knowledge base
- Study: https://docs.stripe.com · https://tailwindcss.com/docs · https://react.dev · https://developers.cloudflare.com
  · https://supabase.com/docs
- Direction: search first; a stable three-column layout (nav, content, on-this-page); copyable code; every page
  answers one question.
- Must have: search, getting started, guides, reference, a "copy page as Markdown" link, llms.txt.

## Dashboard or web app
- Study: https://linear.app · https://ui.shadcn.com/examples/dashboard · https://tremor.so · https://mobbin.com and
  https://refero.design (real app screens)
- Direction: content density with calm chrome; consistent table, filter and form patterns; keyboard support;
  empty, loading and error states designed as carefully as the full state.
- Must have: navigation, list and detail views, forms with validation, settings, empty states, notifications.
- Components: shadcn/ui + ReUI or coss ui for tables and forms; shadcn charts or Tremor.
